## How to send

One email per page, or batches of three, referencing the change request already with them. Say:

> New page for the site. Below is the finished title, meta description, URL, page content and
> image direction, plus the links that need adding to existing pages. Please build as written.

**The internal links matter and get skipped.** Each packet ends with links that must be added to
pages that already exist. On Hibu those are separate edits. A new page with no links pointing at
it will not rank, so treat that section as part of the build, not a nice-to-have.

---

## The accordion instruction

Several packets mark sections as **[ACCORDION]**. That means the section heading is visible and
the content is collapsed until clicked. If Hibu's template cannot do accordions, tell them to
build the sections as normal expanded content — it costs nothing in rankings, the page is just
longer. Do not let anyone cut the content to make it fit.
