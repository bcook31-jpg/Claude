## [ACCORDION] What an accounting clean up involves

Every engagement is different, but the work is usually some combination of these.

- **Reconciling every account, month by month.** Bank, credit card, loans and merchant processors, brought back into agreement with the statements.
- **Recording what never got entered.** Missing transactions, cash spending, owner draws and transfers that were never categorized.
- **Fixing what got entered wrong.** Duplicates, miscategorized expenses, personal spending mixed into the business, and transactions posted to the wrong year.
- **Rebuilding the chart of accounts** when the existing one has grown into something nobody can read.
- **Bringing the accounting records back into a state your return can actually be prepared from**, which is the point of the whole exercise.
- **Untangling the balance sheet.** Undeposited funds, uncleared checks and mystery balances carried forward for years.
- **Producing the year-end financials** your tax return is prepared from.

Most people arrive here because a deadline is coming. Once the books are current, two things usually follow: the return gets filed from real numbers instead of estimates, and monthly bookkeeping keeps it from happening again.

## [ACCORDION] Why books fall behind

Not because the owner is disorganized. Usually because the business got busier than the system built for it, a bookkeeper left, software changed, or a single messy month never got finished and the next one landed on top of it.

We are not going to ask why it happened. We are going to ask for the statements.

## FAQ — questions visible, answers collapsed

**How many years behind can you go?**
As far back as you need. We prepare prior-year and late returns, so the books and the filings can be brought current together.

**Do I need to have receipts for everything?**
No. Bank and card statements establish what was spent. Receipts matter most for large purchases, assets and anything that would be questioned in an audit. IRS Publication 583 sets out what a small business is expected to keep, and we will tell you where your records are thin.

**Can you work in my existing QuickBooks file, or do you start fresh?**
Usually we work in your existing file. Starting fresh is occasionally the faster and cheaper path when the file has years of compounding errors, and we will tell you which one you are looking at before any work starts.

**Will this help me file a late return?**
Yes, and the two are usually done together. Clean books are what a late return gets prepared from, and we prepare prior-year and late returns as well as current ones.

**What if I am not sure how far behind I am?**
That is normal, and finding out is the first thing we do. Send what you have.

**Do you do this for businesses outside California?**
Yes. Clean up work is done remotely from your statements and file, so location does not change what is possible.
