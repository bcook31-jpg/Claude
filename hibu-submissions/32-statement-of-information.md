## [ACCORDION] When yours is due

[VERIFY the whole of this section at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov before publishing.]

- **LLCs** file every two years, in the month the entity was registered.
- **Corporations** file every year, in the month the entity was registered.
- **New entities** file an initial Statement of Information within 90 days of registering.

The state does not reliably remind you, and the deadline is tied to your registration month rather than the calendar or tax year — which is why so many people miss it.

**Not sure when yours is due?** Tell us the entity name and we will look it up. No charge for that.

## [ACCORDION] What happens if you miss it

The Secretary of State can assess a penalty, and an entity that stays delinquent can be suspended. A suspended entity cannot legally do business in California, cannot bring a lawsuit, and can have trouble with banks and contracts. It is fixable, and it is far cheaper not to get there.

## [ACCORDION] Doing it yourself

You can absolutely file this yourself on the Secretary of State's website at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov, and if you are comfortable doing that, you should.

What you are paying us for is that it gets done, on time, by someone tracking the date — and that the details match what is on your tax return. The most common problem we see is not a missed filing. It is a Statement of Information that disagrees with the return, usually because an address or an officer changed and only one of the two got updated.
