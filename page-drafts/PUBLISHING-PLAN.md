# The 19-page publishing plan

**Budget confirmed 2026-09-07: 19 pages.** Writing window is now to the end of December, roughly
16 weeks, because production stops when the season starts in January.

---

## ⛔ This is not the same 19 as the original deadline set

The first list was pages 1 to 19 of the map, which included **seven speculative city pages** that
are still gated on local material you may not have. Now that the map has 35 pages and the invoice
data has surfaced four strong new ones, several better candidates exist.

**Three changes from the original list:**

1. **Page 5 does not consume a slot.** `/business-accounting` already exists, so it is a rewrite
   of a live page rather than a new build. That frees a slot.
2. **Page 7 is dropped.** Once the homepage is retitled to target tax preparation in Lakewood,
   page 7 competes with it and loses — the homepage carries 35 of your 36 ranking keywords.
   Another slot freed.
3. **Six city pages come out, six stronger pages go in.** The city pages that fail the
   three-item local test are worth less than page 32 at 4,400 searches or page 33 at 1,600.

## The city pages

Only **page 8, Long Beach**, made the slate, and only conditionally. It is the one city page with
measurable volume and a real difficulty score.

**If you cannot supply three real local items for Long Beach** — a named client or job, a local
review, something true about working there that would be wrong for the next city — then swap it
for **page 28, Enrolled agent or CPA** (1,300 searches, difficulty 21), which needs nothing from
you but the writing.

The other six city pages stay unbuilt. Add each city as a paragraph and a link inside page 4's or
page 7's "areas we serve" section instead. That is the better outcome, not a compromise: seven
thin city pages mostly do not get indexed and they drag the quality signal across the whole site
while they sit there.

## Pace

19 pages across 16 weeks is **a little over one page a week**, and Hibu is building them, not you.
Two things follow:

- **Ask Hibu their turnaround per page before you commit to this.** If they take two weeks a page
  and work sequentially, 19 is not achievable by January and the plan becomes Wave 1 plus as much
  of Wave 2 as fits.
- **Send them in batches of three or four with finished copy**, not one at a time and not all
  nineteen at once. Include the title, meta description, full copy, image direction and the
  internal links that need adding to existing pages.

**Wave 1 is the one that matters.** If everything slips, six pages live and aged by January beats
nineteen pages published in February, because the February versions miss the season entirely and
wait a year.
