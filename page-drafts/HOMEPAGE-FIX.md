# Homepage title fix

**Current:** `IRS Tax Office in Lakewood, CA | Top Tax Pros`

That claims you are an office of the Internal Revenue Service. You are a CTEC-registered preparer
with limited representation rights. Change it before anything else on this list.

---

## Recommended

```
Tax Preparation & Bookkeeping in Lakewood, CA | Top Tax Pros
```

59 characters, so it will not truncate. It names both service lines, the city, and the brand, in
the order a searcher scans. It is also the honest version of what the current title was reaching
for — the current one ranks you for tax-office searches by claiming something untrue, and this one
earns the same searchers by describing what you do.

**Alternatives if you want a different emphasis:**

| Title | Chars | Best when |
|---|---|---|
| `Tax Preparation & Bookkeeping in Lakewood, CA \| Top Tax Pros` | 59 | Recommended. Both lines, city, brand. |
| `Top Tax Pros \| Tax Preparation & Bookkeeping in Lakewood, CA` | 59 | Brand-led. Better if most of your traffic already searches your name. |
| `Tax Preparation in Lakewood, CA \| Top Tax Pros` | 46 | Tightest focus. Drops bookkeeping, which you are trying to grow. |

**Meta description** (none is currently doing any work):

> Tax preparation and bookkeeping in Lakewood, CA. Open weeknights and Saturdays, year-round.
> Se habla español. Most personal returns $295. Over 20 years local.

157 characters. Every element is a confirmed differentiator: the hours, the language, a real price
and the tenure. Nothing in it is a claim you cannot support.

## Two more title problems on the same site

Search also surfaced these existing pages:

- `/about` — titled **"Tax Preparation & Accounting Lakewood, Ca"**
- `/business-accounting` — a live page with "accounting" in the title and the URL

Both use "accounting" to describe the firm, which is the California restriction we worked through
for page 5. Under *Moore v. California State Bd. of Accountancy* the term is usable alongside a
disclaimer that dispels confusion about licence status, and neither page is likely to carry one
today.

**Cheapest fix:** retitle `/about` to "About Top Tax Pros | Tax Preparation in Lakewood, CA" and
add the page 5 disclaimer to `/business-accounting`, or retitle that one to "Small Business
Bookkeeping & Tax Services". Changing a live URL has its own cost, so the disclaimer is probably
the lighter option there.

## ⚠️ One thing to decide alongside this

Your homepage currently carries **35 of your 36 ranking keywords**. If the homepage title targets
"tax preparation Lakewood" and page 7 also targets it, the two compete — and on a site with
Authority Score 9 the homepage wins, which makes page 7 redundant.

**Two clean ways to split it:**

1. **Homepage takes the broad term, page 7 becomes the office page.** Homepage: tax preparation
   and bookkeeping in Lakewood. Page 7: appointments, hours, directions, what to bring, the people.
   Different intent, no collision.
2. **Drop page 7 entirely** and let the homepage be the tax preparation page. Saves a slot against
   the Hibu page budget, which may matter more than the page does.

I would take option 1 if you have the page budget and option 2 if you do not. Either way, do not
publish page 7 with the same title focus as a retitled homepage.
