## ⛔ Two things to look at before any new page ships

### 1. Your homepage title tag reads "IRS Tax Office in Lakewood, CA | Top Tax Pros"

That is the title Google shows in the results, and it is the first thing a searcher reads about
you. "IRS Tax Office" states that you are an office of the Internal Revenue Service. You are not.
You are a CTEC-registered preparer, with no representation rights before the IRS at all.

This is not an SEO problem, it is a misrepresentation problem. Implying an affiliation with a
federal agency is a materially different thing from a keyword stuffed into a title. It is also
the kind of claim a competitor reports.

**Change it.** Something like "Tax Preparation in Lakewood, CA | Top Tax Pros" targets the same
searcher honestly. This is a five-minute Hibu edit and I would do it before publishing anything
new.
