## Collapsed sections

### What we fix in the file (accordion)

- **Unreconciled accounts** brought back into agreement with the statements, month by month
- **Duplicate transactions** from double-imported bank feeds
- **Miscategorized expenses**, including personal spending posted to the business
- **Undeposited Funds** cleared out and the deposits matched to the right payments
- **Uncleared checks and stale transactions** carried forward for years
- **Accounts receivable and payable** aged correctly, so the reports mean something
- **A chart of accounts** rebuilt when it has sprawled into duplicates and dead accounts
- **Opening balances and prior-year figures** tied back to the filed return

### How the review works (accordion)

1. You give us read-only access to the file, or send a backup.
2. We look at the reconciliation status of every account, the balance sheet, and the last twelve
   months of transactions.
3. You get a written summary: what is wrong, how far back it goes, what it takes to fix, and
   what it costs.
4. You decide. No obligation.

### If the file is beyond repair (accordion)

Occasionally it is faster and cheaper to start a clean file and bring the balances forward than
to repair years of compounding errors. We will tell you when that is the case, and we will tell
you why. It is not the answer we reach for first.

## FAQ (questions visible, answers collapsed)

**Do you need my login?**
Read-only accountant access is enough to review the file. We will tell you exactly what to send
and how to grant it.

**How far back do you go?**
As far as the return needs. Cleanup usually runs to the last filed year, so the numbers tie back
to something already on record.

**Will this fix my reports?**
Fixing the reports is the point. A profit and loss statement is only as good as the
categorization behind it, which is why bad reports are almost always a data problem, not a
software problem.

**Can you keep it clean afterward?**
Yes, that is monthly bookkeeping. [Link to page 4.] Most cleanup clients move onto it, because
the file drifts again within a year otherwise.
