## Collapsed sections

### When yours is due (accordion)

⚠️ **[VERIFY the whole of this section against the Secretary of State before publishing.** The
general shape below is right, but the specifics are exactly what a reader will act on.]

- **LLCs** file every two years, in the month the entity was registered.
- **Corporations** file every year, in the month the entity was registered.
- **New entities** file an initial Statement of Information within 90 days of registering.

The state does not reliably remind you, and the deadline is tied to your registration month rather
than the calendar or tax year — which is why so many people miss it.

**Not sure when yours is due?** Tell us the entity name and we will look it up. No charge for that.

### What happens if you miss it (accordion)

The Secretary of State can assess a penalty, and an entity that stays delinquent can be suspended.
A suspended entity cannot legally do business in California, cannot bring a lawsuit, and can have
trouble with banks and contracts. It is fixable, and it is far cheaper not to get there.

⚠️ [Verify the current penalty amount and the suspension process before printing them.]

### What we need from you (accordion)

- Entity name and entity number
- Current business address and mailing address
- Names and addresses of officers, directors or managing members
- Registered agent details
- A brief description of the business

That is it. Most of it we can pull from the state's own record if you do not have it to hand.

### Doing it yourself (accordion)

You can absolutely file this yourself on the Secretary of State's website, and if you are
comfortable doing that, you should. https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/

What you are paying us for is that it gets done, on time, by someone tracking the date — and that
the details match what is on your tax return. The most common problem we see is not a missed
filing, it is a Statement of Information that disagrees with the return, usually because an
address or an officer changed and only one of the two got updated.
