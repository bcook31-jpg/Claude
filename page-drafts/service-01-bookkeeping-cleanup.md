## Collapsed sections

### What cleanup actually involves (accordion)

Every engagement is different, but the work is usually some combination of these.

- **Reconciling every account, month by month.** Bank, credit card, loans and merchant
  processors, brought back into agreement with the statements.
- **Recording what never got entered.** Missing transactions, cash spending, owner draws and
  transfers that were never categorized.
- **Fixing what got entered wrong.** Duplicates, miscategorized expenses, personal spending
  mixed into the business, and transactions posted to the wrong year.
- **Rebuilding the chart of accounts** when the existing one has grown into something nobody
  can read.
- **Untangling the balance sheet.** Undeposited funds, uncleared checks and mystery balances
  that have been carried forward for years.
- **Producing the year-end financials** your tax return is prepared from.

### Why books fall behind (accordion)

Not because the owner is disorganized. Usually because the business got busier than the
system built for it, a bookkeeper left, software changed, or a single messy month never got
finished and the next one landed on top of it.

We are not going to ask why it happened. We are going to ask for the statements.

## FAQ (questions visible, answers collapsed)

**How many years behind can you go?**
[NEEDS FROM YOU: your actual limit.] As a rule, the further back you go the more the work is
reconstruction from statements rather than cleanup of existing records, and the cost reflects
that.

**Do I need to have receipts for everything?**
No. Bank and card statements establish what was spent. Receipts matter most for large purchases,
assets and anything that would be questioned in an audit. IRS Publication 583 sets out what a
small business is expected to keep and why, and we will tell you where your records are thin.
[Link: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p583.pdf]

**Can you work in my existing QuickBooks file, or do you start fresh?**
Usually we work in your existing file. Starting fresh is occasionally the faster and cheaper
path when the file has years of compounding errors, and we will tell you which one you are
looking at before any work starts.

**Will this help me file a late return?**
Clean books are what a late return gets prepared from, so yes, and the two are usually done
together. [NEEDS FROM YOU: confirm whether you prepare late and prior-year returns. If not, this
answer changes and the page should not imply it.]

**What if I am not sure how far behind I am?**
That is normal, and finding out is the first thing we do. Send what you have.

**Do you do this for businesses outside California?**
Yes. Cleanup work is done remotely from your statements and file, so location does not change
what is possible.
