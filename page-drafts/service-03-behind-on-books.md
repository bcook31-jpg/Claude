## Collapsed sections

### If the deadline is this week (accordion)

Say so when you call. What we can usually do at short notice:

- Reconcile the accounts that the return actually depends on, first
- Get you a defensible set of numbers to file from
- File an extension where one is available, so the rest of the cleanup happens without a clock
  on it

*Confirmed: we file extensions.*

An extension moves the filing date, not the payment date. Interest and penalties still accrue on
tax owed from the original due date, and anyone who tells you otherwise is costing you money.

### If the deadline already passed (accordion)

Still fixable, and still worth doing quickly, because most late-filing penalties accrue monthly.
The books get caught up, the return gets prepared from real numbers, and you stop the meter.

*Confirmed: we prepare prior-year and late returns.*

## FAQ (questions visible, answers collapsed)

**How fast can you actually do this?**
Catch-up work is finished in 14 business days or less from receiving your statements. How far back
we are going and how many accounts are involved move that.

**I have years, not months. Is that too far gone?**
No. It changes the price and the timeline, not whether it is possible.

**I do not have receipts.**
Statements establish what was spent. Receipts matter most for large purchases and assets. IRS
Publication 583 sets out what a small business is expected to keep.
[https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p583.pdf]

**Can you talk to the IRS for me?**
No. We prepare books and returns. Representation before the IRS is not something we offer, and
we will tell you plainly when that is what you need.
