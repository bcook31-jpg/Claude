## ⛔ 1. The gap that limits page 4

**Primary category is "Tax preparation". There is no bookkeeping category at all.**

Page 4 targets `bookkeeping services near me` — 6,600 searches a month, the largest volume on the
map. Near-me queries are answered from the map pack, and **the primary category is the single
biggest input into which map packs you appear in.** A profile categorized only as tax preparation
and tax consultant is competing for bookkeeping queries with one hand tied.

Listing "Bookkeeping" under *services* is not the same thing. Services describe what you do once
Google has already decided you are relevant. Categories decide whether you are relevant at all.

**The fix: add "Bookkeeping service" as an additional category.** Keep "Tax preparation" as
primary — that is correct, it is 82% of your invoices and it drives your season. Additional
categories make you eligible for more queries without displacing the primary one.

This is a two-minute change and it is worth more to page 4 than anything I can write on the page.

**Consider also adding "Accountant"** — ⚠️ but only after reading item 2 below. Google's category
list includes it, and it maps to real search demand, but it is the restricted term in California.
My recommendation is to skip it. Bookkeeping service covers the demand you can serve without the
exposure.

---

## ⛔ 2. Two service entries use the restricted word

Your services list includes **"Business Accounting"** and **"Accounting"**.

This is the same California issue we worked through for page 5 and the `/business-accounting`
page: B&P Code 5058, narrowed by *Moore v. California State Bd. of Accountancy* (1992), permits
"accounting" only where a modifier or disclaimer dispels confusion about licence status. A Google
Business Profile service entry is a bare label with nowhere to put a disclaimer.

**Rename both.** Suggested:

- "Business Accounting" → **"Small Business Bookkeeping"**
- "Accounting" → **"Monthly Bookkeeping"**

You lose nothing. The searches those entries were reaching for are covered by the bookkeeping
category and by the service entries you keep.
