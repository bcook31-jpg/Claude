### What we do (accordion)

- **Entity selection** — LLC, S corporation, C corporation or partnership, chosen against how you
  will actually be taxed rather than what sounds best

- **Initial Statement of Information**, due within 90 days of registering
- **Books set up from day one**, so the first return is prepared from real records

### Choosing the entity type (accordion)

This is the decision worth spending time on, and it is the one the filing services do not help
with. An LLC is simple to form and flexible to run. An S corporation election can reduce
self-employment tax once profit is high enough, at the cost of running payroll and filing a
separate return. A partnership has its own filing and its own deadlines.

Which is right depends on your expected profit, whether you have partners, and whether you want to
take a salary. We will walk through it before anything is filed, because changing it afterwards
costs more than getting it right.

## FAQ

**Do I need a lawyer to form an LLC?**
Usually not. Formation is a filing, and a firm that does them regularly can handle it. A lawyer is
worth it where there are multiple partners with different contributions, outside investors,
property, or anything likely to end up in a written agreement you would want enforced.

**Can I just use an online filing service?**
You can, and they are cheaper. What they do not do is help you choose the entity type, set up the
books, file the Statement of Information on time, or prepare the return. If you want the filing
only, use them. If you want the twelve months after the filing, that is what we do.
